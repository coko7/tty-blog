# Welcome to `The Terminal Yard`

I'm coko. A french developer who's passionate about programming and open-source.

I spend most of my time in the terminal and rely on a variety of command-line tools to help me on my day job:
[neovim](https://neovim.io/), [tmux](https://github.com/tmux/tmux), [fzf](https://github.com/junegunn/fzf), [sed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sed), [rg](https://github.com/BurntSushi/ripgrep), [jq](https://github.com/jqlang/jq), [git](https://git-scm.com), [curl](https://curl.se/), [xh](https://github.com/ducaale/xh), [ssh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secure_Shell), *and many more...*

I also like writing, which is why I started this blog as an experiment.
The curious-looking contraption I wear on my head is a [Corne keyboard](https://github.com/foostan/crkbd). Here's a close-up:

![Corne keyboard with Ergo-L key layout](./images/crkbd.png)

~I don't actually wear it on my head~ but if you want to know more about how I use this thing, it is documented on my [GitHub](https://github.com/coko7/crkbd).
